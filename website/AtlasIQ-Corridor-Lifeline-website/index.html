01 / THE PURPOSEAUSTRALIA'S EAST–WEST CONNECTION

When the corridor stops,
the impact keeps moving.

Across freight teams, warehouses and supply chains, a disruption becomes a question of what happens next. The information needed to make that decision is often scattered.

THE IDEA IS SIMPLE

Less searching.
More context.

Bring weather observations, public warnings and verified rail information together. Show what is known, how current it is and where information is missing.

Current prototype

A clearer picture
starts with the inputs.

The first prototype brings configured weather observations, public warnings and manually verified rail status into one explainable summary. Visible rules show the reasons behind the result.

A rule score gives context. It is not a probability or a prediction of closure.

03 / THE BUILDEVIDENCE BEFORE EXPANSION

A working
starting point.

Corridor Lifeline is early. The foundation exists; the work now is to understand its limits and build responsibly from there.

01 / AVAILABLE TODAY

Local prototype.

Configured inputs, transparent rules and reviewable summary drafts. Offline demonstrations show the software's behaviour using illustrative historical scenarios.

02 / STILL TO VALIDATE

Coverage. Permissions. Performance.

Broader corridor coverage and historical testing remain ahead. No advance-warning lead time or closure-prediction accuracy has been demonstrated.

03 / THE PROPOSED DIRECTION

Reviewed alerts.
Paperwork support.

Develop dependable subscriber delivery and explore emergency documentation drafts, with people reviewing the information and approving the next step.
