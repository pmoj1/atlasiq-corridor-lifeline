The problem

The back office is where freight slows down.

Every shipment out of Australia drags a paper trail behind it — bills of lading, commercial invoices, packing lists, certificates of origin — stacked with Australian Border Force and biosecurity clearance rules. All of it typed, checked and re-checked by hand.

  • Brokers re-type the same shipment details from emails, PDFs and spreadsheets, all day.
  • A 20-line carrier invoice averages at least one wrong field — invisible until a dispute surfaces.
  • One misclassified tariff code can hold a container at the wharf for days.

The product

One engine. Two capabilities.

We start narrow and prove ROI with real customers — then extend the same engine into the part nobody else handles: Australian export compliance.

Part A · Order entry — the easy sell

AtlasIQ Extract

An AI agent reads incoming shipment requests — emails, PDFs, spreadsheets from shippers or exporters — and automatically builds the load record: cargo details, weight, dimensions, origin/destination, pricing lookup. Stop retyping shipment details.

Part B · Compliance — our actual moat

AtlasIQ Comply

The same extraction engine drafts the certificate of origin, biosecurity declaration, correct tariff/HS classification and bill of lading — flagging mismatches before they cause a customs hold or a shipment claim.
