# Brag Plan: AtlasIQ

## Planning rubric
1. A local east–west freight workspace for reviewing public notices, evidence and supply scenarios.
2. Gaps never become an all-clear; source health stays visible.
3. The real forest-and-ivory notice desk and amber navigation.
4. Actual notice desk screenshot, then source-health and supply scenario screens from the local app.
5. 22 seconds, landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps.
6. Polished: an operational product film with restrained typography and purposeful camera moves.
7. Bundled Business Moves vol-12, quiet warm bed; two soft motion accents, no narration.
8. I’m building AtlasIQ Corridor Lifeline: a local workspace for reviewing east–west freight notices, tracing sources and exploring supply-delay scenarios. Prototype in development.
9. Review notice queue → inspect source evidence → explore stock and delay assumptions.

## Creative angle
The corridor is long. The evidence should be within reach. Show the working local prototype and its traceability without promising predictive accuracy, alerts or commercial coverage. Keep “Local prototype” on screen. No closure-review or Nyanzaga material.

## Visual identity
LOOK: ground #111d1c; paper #f5f5ef; ink #162221; accent #edb85f; Space Grotesk 500/700 from existing brand font; 96px headline / 32px labels; 96px safe margin; 2px borders; real product screens occupy 70% of frame.
BANNED: invented success metrics, all-clear state, prediction claims, customer names, marketing-only dashboard mockups, voiceover, generic particles.

## Storyboard
### 1 — The corridor — 0–3.27s
Hook: “When the corridor stops,” then “the impact keeps moving.” Use existing site wording. Short schematic Perth–Port Augusta line draws beneath it. Local prototype label. Soft music entry; no sequential small print.
### 2 — Review — 3.27–10.93s
Headline “Review the notice.” Actual notice desk screenshot enters as a product surface. Label “Saved screen · 8 November 2026”. Camera reframes toward the queue, a cursor moves to Report detail; no pretence of a current live check. Original screenshot numbers are historical interface content, not new performance claims.
### 3 — Trace and plan — 10.93–18.56s
Actual evidence screen appears with “Trace the source.” Then actual supply scenario screen with “Explore the delay.” An amber tab focus and soft click support each change. Keep scenario illustrative label and stale/unknown evidence visible. No fabricated input results.
### 4 — Brand — 18.56–22s
“AtlasIQ” and “Corridor Lifeline.” Final line “Local prototype. Under development.” Hold at least 2.5 seconds, music fades over last 1.2s.

## Music cue guidance
Business Moves vol-12 by ende.app, CC BY 4.0; about 109.96 BPM. Bundled cue preset read. Main changes at 10.93 and 18.56 seconds; use natural timing elsewhere for readability. No audio-reactive effect if extraction unavailable. SFX sparse and motion-matched.

## Claim boundaries
Rail status remains manually verified. Screens show saved or illustrative states. No current incident verification, predictive validation, delivered alerts, deployment or customer outcomes asserted.
