# AtlasIQ — Before the disruption

Create an exactly 30.000-second cinematic brand film for AtlasIQ's Corridor Lifeline Agent concept. Output 1920×1080, 30 fps, 900 frames. Tell one human-scale story: a shipment crosses an immense, exposed Australian corridor; the weather changes; scattered signals resolve into a clear warning; a person gets time and prepared paperwork to plan a response. The emotional arc is momentum → uncertainty → understanding → preparation → composure.

## Art direction

Use the craft of a premium technology keynote film: confident simplicity, monumental but sparse typography, exquisitely timed transitions, tactile photographic detail, deep blacks and restrained light. Keep AtlasIQ's own identity. Do not copy Apple logos, product interfaces, devices, slogans or sound marks. Near-black navy #071522, warm amber #FFA028, off-white #EAF0F9, muted slate and a small teal review cue. Space Grotesk, original website logo proportions and geometry.

Combine photoreal Australian freight imagery with designed motion graphics. Long, low horizons, credible road trains and freight rail, vast limestone scrub country, storm light, damp asphalt, physically plausible scale. Use slow travelling camera moves, multi-plane depth, subtle atmospheric movement and a delicate horizontal anamorphic flare. Reserve optical effects for light sources and scene transitions. No particle overload or generic AI imagery.

Compose full 16:9 without permanent black bars. Keep key titles and workflow close to centre for a later vertical recomposition. Titles should breathe. One thought at a time. No small dashboard clutter. Shapes and camera motion should connect the scenes rather than making a slideshow.
