# Image-generation prompts

All five assets used the built-in image-generation tool. No CLI/API fallback was used. The high-level creative direction is in ../MASTER_PROMPT.md. These photographic assets are illustrative generated scenes.

## freight-dawn.png

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one cinematic photoreal establishing plate for a premium Australian freight technology brand film. 16:9 landscape composition, 1920x1080 or larger. The real-world scene is the Nullarbor Plain in Australia: immense flat dry limestone country, sparse low saltbush, a straight two-lane asphalt highway receding toward the horizon. A credible silver-white Australian conventional-cab road train with two trailers travels toward the camera, small-to-medium in the lower right half, three-quarter front view, headlights softly on. Low sweeping drone camera around 3 metres high looking down the road; horizon at upper third. Soft golden dawn edge light from far right, navy storm-blue shadows, subtle horizontal anamorphic highlight from headlights and beautiful realistic optical depth. Upper left and central sky offer clean quiet negative space for large white type. Luxury documentary cinematography, tactile road texture, physically plausible truck and wheels, muted colours, cinematic exposure with readable dark tones. Not a glossy cartoon, not concept art. No text, no logos, no watermark, no UI, no border or letterbox. This is a single coherent shot, not a collage. Designed to animate later with a slow camera push and subtle atmosphere.

## freight-storm.png

Use case: ads-marketing. Single photoreal cinematic aerial shot for a luxury Australian freight technology film, landscape 16:9. View obliquely down a long freight rail line crossing the immense flat Australian Nullarbor Plain; a long realistic Australian diesel freight train is modestly sized in lower third travelling toward a distant vanishing point. A parallel straight asphalt highway is visible well separated from the rails. Sparse low saltbush, flat limestone earth, no mountains or city. A vast slate-blue storm front occupies the upper half, curtains of distant rain in the far distance, a narrow amber light break at the horizon; realistic damp-dark earth with no flood or destruction. Restrained navy/grey palette with a trace of warm amber. Premium documentary cinematography, shot with anamorphic optics, detailed but calm, negative space upper-left for typography. Clear plausible rail geometry and believable train scale. No text, logos, watermarks, map graphics or UI. No collage. Compose for a slow cinematic camera move later.

## road-clean.png

Edit target: freight-dawn.png. Use case: precise-object-edit. This image is the edit target for a premium motion film. Remove ONLY the silver road train and all its trailers and their cast shadows/reflections, replacing that area with the naturally continuing empty asphalt highway and adjacent desert. Preserve the camera framing, horizon, road lane markings and curves, land, clouds, sunset, colours, lighting and all other geometry as precisely as possible. Empty road clean background plate for compositing the vehicle back in as a moving independent layer. Keep same landscape aspect ratio. No new objects, no text.

## truck-cutout.png

Edit target: freight-dawn.png. Transparent background requested. Use case: background-extraction. Extract ONLY the exact silver-white road train in this image, including cab, both trailers, wheels and exhaust pipes. Preserve the source vehicle appearance, all proportions, three-quarter-front viewing angle and golden lighting precisely. Remove absolutely all environment, road, sky and ground. Output the complete intact truck on a truly transparent background, with a close bounding box around the whole vehicle. Do not invent or redesign any parts, do not add text. The image is a compositing cutout for the original scene.

## operations.png

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a photoreal cinematic background plate for a premium Australian freight technology launch film. 16:9 landscape. In a quiet contemporary freight operations office at blue hour, a close low camera looks along an elegant charcoal desk. A softly out-of-focus unbranded closed notebook and blank white shipment papers sit at lower right; a human operator's hand lightly resting on the papers, sleeve of a navy work shirt, no face visible. Through a wide window in the background, parked trucks and a warehouse at dusk form very soft realistic bokeh, warm amber practical lights. The centre and left of frame must be dark negative space for a large composited floating notification interface. Photographic realism, tactile paper, believable fingers, cinematic anamorphic optics and shallow depth of field, premium restrained navy/amber grade. No screens with content, no words or numbers, no logos, no watermark, no diagram, no glowing AI graphics, no border. One coherent photographic frame.
